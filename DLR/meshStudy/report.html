DLR campus: mesh-independence study

Status: Done: none yet. Still running/queued: coarse, medium, fine.

Why we do this. Each cell stores one averaged value, so a coarse mesh smears out the flow. We halve the cell size around a few buildings (2 m → 1 m → 0.5 m) and watch the probes. When halving again barely changes them, the remaining error comes from the physics model (RANS k-ε, inflow), not from the grid. The change medium → fine column is the key number. GCI is the standard error band on the fine result (Roache): below about 5 % is usually accepted. The controls (upstream, above campus) are outside the refined box: they should not move. If they do, something else changed.
